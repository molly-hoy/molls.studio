Every crafting tool you need, in one place. Free.

Hi, I’m Moll. I built this studio because crafting shouldn’t cost a fortune before you’ve even cast on. Every tool I wished I had is here: chart makers, patterns, calculators and trackers. Save your designs, come back to them whenever you like, edit them and export them. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no “upgrade to pro”.

Whether you’re picking up your needles or hook for the very first time, or finding your way back to a craft you fell out of love with, you’re welcome here.

Materials cost calculator

Add what a project needs, what one pack costs and what you already own. It works out how many packs to buy, the total cost and the cost of each garment.

1. your project

The extra is added to yarn and fabric only (metres, yards, grams and cm), to cover tension differences and mistakes.

2. materials for one garment

For each material, enter how much one garment needs, what comes in one pack (a skein, a card of buttons) and its price. Leave “already have” empty if you need to buy everything.

3. the cost

4. my shopping list
